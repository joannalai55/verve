# Polishing and persona

## Select the register

| Situation | Optimize for | Avoid |
|---|---|---|
| Work message | clarity, brevity, calm ownership | hedging, faux authority, needless warmth |
| Pitch | tension, stakes, contrast, memorable close | inflated claims, slogan piles |
| Presentation | spoken syntax, signposting, cadence | essay-like sentences |
| Casual chat | ease, responsiveness, light specificity | polished monologues |
| Social post | point of view, texture, concise surprise | generic hype, trend-word stuffing |
| Flirting | attention, play, plausible deniability, respect | pressure, scripted intimacy |
| Playful conflict | precision, humor, an exit ramp | humiliation, punching down |

## Use persona controls

Treat admired speakers and characters as coordinates, not voices to clone:

- **Sharp**: lead with the decision or observation; remove throat-clearing.
- **Compelling**: create contrast, vary sentence length, connect stakes to people.
- **Charming**: notice something specific, leave room for the other person, add warmth.
- **Dry and incisive**: state the unexpected inference plainly; do not explain the joke twice.
- **Provocative**: challenge the premise, not the person's dignity.

Use no more than two dominant controls in one response. Constant intensity sounds performed.

## Diagnose high-value friction

Prioritize corrections in this order:

1. meaning or social effect;
2. collocation and natural phrasing;
3. information order and rhythm;
4. grammar patterns that recur;
5. minor grammar that does not obstruct meaning.

Explain a correction as a reusable contrast. A concrete verb often beats an intensifier plus a generic verb. In chat, a fragment can sound more natural than a complete sentence.

Avoid calling one wording "what natives say" when several registers or regions differ.

## Calibrate edge

Before producing bold, flirtatious, or cutting language, assess:

- relationship closeness;
- power difference;
- public versus private setting;
- reversibility if misunderstood;
- whether warmth is already established.

When context is thin, give a socially safe default and label the riskier option. Explain the likely signal in plain language.

## Preserve the user's voice

Retain their emotional temperature, sentence length preference, directness, and distinctive imagery when these work. If a phrase is unusual but evocative, improve its framing instead of flattening it into generic fluency.
